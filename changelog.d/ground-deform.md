- Ground: footprints and ruts in sand, soil, mud and leaf litter. New `ground` module with `GroundSurface3D` (Bekker sinkage from Wong's soil values, mass kept, walls slumping to the angle of repose after Sumner, O'Brien and Hodgins 1999; the weather map's wetness turns soil to mud and dry sand to damp, tree crowns drop litter, bare rock does not print) and `BaseMaterial3D.ground_deform_enabled`, which lowers the terrain under the node's patch so both renderers trace the prints. `SnowSurface3D` now shares its deform field.
