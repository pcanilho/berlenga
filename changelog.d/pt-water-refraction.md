### Added
- Transmission bounds a medium when `transmission_thin_walled` is off: `transmission_ior`, `transmission_attenuation_color` and `transmission_attenuation_distance` (glTF `KHR_materials_volume` and `KHR_materials_ior`, imported and exported). The path tracer refracts rays into and out of it with total internal reflection and absorbs every ray inside it (Beer-Lambert), shadow rays included, so water darkens and tints with depth; the rasterizer refracts the screen read by the IOR and tints it by the depth behind. Setting `rendering/pathtracing/refraction` (#N)
