- Snow: `SnowCover3D` steps its cells, slides, drift and cornices (and the world tier's turn) on a worker thread and swaps the results in when they are done (`background_updates`, on by default; `sync_update()` for tests). The pack is bit-identical to updating in the frame; the main thread's share of an update drops from about 6 ms to about 1 ms, so the twice-a-second hitch is gone.
