- Snow: the wind moves lying snow. Above the transport threshold (by temperature and density) snow leaves windward shoulders and crests and settles below them in the lee, wind-packed; the wind over the ground follows its slope and curvature. SnowTran-3D mass balance with Pomeroy saltation and suspension. Frozen crusts, wet snow and snow under trees stay. `SnowCover3D.wind_transport_enabled`, `transport_flux()`.
