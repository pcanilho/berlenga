### Added

- Five broadleaves: aspen, black poplar, wild cherry, hornbeam and sweet chestnut, each with its own form, leaves, bark, seasons and habitat (`TreeGenerator.PRESET_ASPEN` to `PRESET_SWEET_CHESTNUT`).
- Poplar leaves tremble in the lightest air.
- Hornbeam stems and limbs are fluted, and sweet chestnut bark twists round its bole with age.
- Wild cherry flowers white in April.
- Aspen grows in clones: every stem of a clone shares its bark, form, leaf-out and autumn colour, the next clone differs (species meta `clone_scale`).
- Hornbeam grows under the oaks of the mixed oak wood, black poplar on the floodplain, aspen in the taiga's burns.
